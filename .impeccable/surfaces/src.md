# Plotr app UI

Scope: the whole desktop app UI (Start, Dashboard, Folder, Plot Line, Info Map, Notes board, note page, Search, Trash, settings, modals). Mode: Operate. Replaces the "Pine" look (Geist, centred SaaS column, black pill buttons).

Audience and job: the author alone, long evening sessions over months, planning novels, games, scripts and worlds. Find a board, read the story's shape, capture and edit notes.

Unresolved: none blocking. Scope for this round also includes the Plot Line naming regression and keyboard/focus fixes from the 2026-09-29 critique.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Plotr is a book in proof. Every screen is a galley page with a margin; the writer's words are the typeset text and every tool is a proofreader's mark. Refuses the centred dev-tool column and the cream-and-lamplight notebook.

OWN-WORLD: White galley stock, ink #141414, graphite #62626A, hairline rules #E3E3E0, one pine pencil #29524A that means selected, current, act. Category colours live only as small flags. Dark mode is the negative: film black #111214, off-white type, lighter pine. Source Serif 4 sets all content, Schibsted Grotesk sets controls, Courier Prime sets slug lines, numbers, dates, keys. Selection is a pine change bar in the number margin. No cards, no pills as containers: parts are divided by space and rules.

STORY: The writer sees their own titles, loglines and moments first, knows where they are from the running head and slug line, and opens anything with one click or Enter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard at 1280×800: running-head top bar; left-aligned galley column; typewritten slug line; project title in 44px serif; italic logline; contents list with numbers in the margin, board preview, serif name, dot leaders, type and date in typescript.

FORM: The Galley Proof, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (pick card), seed key 724ef032. Signature interaction: the Plot Line moment card hangs from its dot on a pine leader line, like an author query; arrows step moment to moment, Enter opens the note as a full-height page. Motion grammar: the note page slides in once from the right, exponential ease-out, off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
