# Plotr Privacy Policy

Plotr is a personal, offline-first desktop application for writers. One person
uses it: its owner.

## What Plotr stores

- All project data (notes, boards, images) stays in local folders on your
  computer. Plotr has no servers.
- Plotr sends no data to the developer or to any third party.

## Pinterest connector

- When you connect Pinterest, Plotr asks for read-only access to your own
  boards and pins (`boards:read`, `pins:read`).
- Plotr fetches board lists and pin images only when you start an import.
- Imported images are saved into your local project folder.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on your computer and are never shared.
- Disconnecting removes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access at any
  time in your Pinterest security settings.

## Contact

Open an issue on this repository for any privacy question.
